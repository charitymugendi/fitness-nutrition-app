Fun Workouts For Kids

Discover exciting exercises and activities designed to keep children active, healthy, and having fun. Each exercise includes simple instructions and focuses on developing coordination, strength, and flexibility.

FUN STRENGTH EXERCISES

Animal Walks

3 sets of 30 seconds each
Easy

Fun animal-inspired movements that build full-body strength and coordination while sparking imagination.

  • Bear Walk: Walk on hands and feet with straight legs
  • Crab Walk: Move backward on hands and feet with belly up
  • Frog Jumps: Squat down and jump forward like a frog
  • Penguin Waddle: Walk with legs together and arms at sides
  • Elephant Swing: Bend forward and swing arms like a trunk

Fun Fact!

Animal walks help develop bilateral coordination - using both sides of the body together!

Full Body Coordination Balance

Superhero Poses

Hold each pose for 15-20 seconds
Easy

Channel inner superheroes with these fun strength poses that build confidence and body awareness.

  • Superman: Lie on stomach, lift arms and legs off ground
  • Wonder Woman: Stand strong with hands on hips
  • Spiderman Crawl: Alternate bringing knees to elbows
  • Hulk Smash: Squat down then jump up with arms overhead
  • Captain America Shield: Hold plank position with one arm extended

Fun Fact!

Superhero poses help children develop core strength which improves posture and balance!

Core Legs Back

Mini Obstacle Course

Complete course 3-5 times
Medium

Create a simple obstacle course using household items to build agility, problem-solving skills, and confidence.

  • Crawl under a table or through a tunnel
  • Step carefully between pillows on the floor
  • Jump over a line of books or small obstacles
  • Balance walk along a taped line on the floor
  • Toss a soft toy into a basket at the finish line

Fun Fact!

Obstacle courses help develop executive function skills like planning and problem-solving!

ACTIVE GAMES & COORDINATION

Active Simon Says

Play for 10-15 minutes
Easy

A classic game with active movements that builds listening skills, coordination, and body control.

  • Simon says touch your toes
  • Simon says do 5 jumping jacks
  • Simon says balance on one foot
  • Simon says do a silly dance
  • Simon says wiggle like a worm

Fun Fact!

Simon Says helps develop inhibitory control - the ability to stop an action when needed!

Full Body Listening Skills Coordination

Balloon Volleyball

Play for 10-15 minutes
Medium

A safe indoor game that develops hand-eye coordination, timing, and teamwork skills.

  • Blow up a balloon and create a "net" with a string or tape
  • Hit the balloon back and forth without letting it touch the ground
  • Try using different body parts to hit the balloon
  • Count how many times you can keep it in the air
  • Challenge siblings or parents to join the fun

Fun Fact!

Balloon volleyball helps develop tracking skills - following objects with your eyes!

Arms Hand-Eye Coordination Teamwork

Freeze Dance

Play for 10-15 minutes
Easy

A high-energy game that combines dancing, listening skills, and body control in a fun format.

  • Play fun, upbeat music
  • Dance freely while the music plays
  • Freeze in place when the music stops
  • Try different dance styles each round
  • Make funny poses when you freeze

Fun Fact!

Freeze dance helps develop auditory processing - connecting what you hear with how you move!

FLEXIBILITY & BALANCE

Kids Yoga Adventure

Hold poses for 15-30 seconds
Easy

Simple yoga poses presented as an adventure story to build flexibility, balance, and body awareness.

  • Downward Dog: Be a triangle-shaped mountain
  • Tree Pose: Balance like a tall tree in the forest
  • Cat-Cow: Arch and round your back like a stretching cat
  • Butterfly Pose: Sit with feet together and flap your wings
  • Child's Pose: Curl up small like a sleepy mouse

Fun Fact!

Yoga helps children develop proprioception - knowing where their body is in space!

Flexibility Balance Core

Balance Challenges

Try each challenge for 20-30 seconds
Medium

Fun balance activities that improve stability, coordination, and focus while having fun with challenges.

  • Flamingo Stand: Balance on one foot like a flamingo
  • Balance Beam: Walk along a line on the floor
  • Statue Game: Balance in different positions without moving
  • Heel-to-Toe Walk: Walk in a straight line placing heel to toe
  • Balance with a Book: Try balancing a book on your head while walking

Fun Fact!

Balance activities strengthen the vestibular system - our internal balance system!

Legs Core Focus

Stretching Games

Hold stretches for 15-20 seconds
Easy

Creative stretching activities that make flexibility training fun and engaging for young bodies.

  • Rainbow Reach: Stretch arms high and side to side like drawing a rainbow
  • Toe Touch Challenge: See how close you can get to touching your toes
  • Alphabet Stretch: Use your body to form different letters
  • Stretching Story: Follow along with a story that includes different stretches
  • Mirror Stretch: Copy a partner's stretching movements

Fun Fact!

Stretching helps muscles grow properly and can prevent injuries during play!
