Workouts For Men

Discover powerful exercises specifically designed for men's fitness goals. Build strength, muscle mass, and athletic performance with these proven training techniques.

STRENGTH & MASS BUILDING

Barbell Squats

4 sets of 6-8 reps

The king of all exercises for building lower body strength and overall muscle mass. Essential for developing powerful legs and core stability.

  • Position barbell across upper back, not neck
  • Keep chest up and back straight throughout movement
  • Descend until thighs are parallel to floor or lower
  • Drive through heels to return to standing position
  • Maintain tight core and neutral spine

Barbell Bench Press

4 sets of 6-10 reps

The ultimate upper body mass builder. Develops chest, shoulders, and triceps for that powerful athletic look.

  • Lie flat on bench with feet firmly on floor
  • Grip barbell slightly wider than shoulder-width
  • Lower bar to mid-chest with control
  • Press bar back to starting position explosively
  • Keep shoulder blades retracted throughout movement
Chest Shoulders Triceps

Conventional Deadlifts

3 sets of 4-6 reps

The ultimate test of total body strength. Builds a powerful back, legs, and grip while stimulating massive hormonal response.

  • Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell over mid-foot
  • Bend at hips and knees to grip the bar
  • Keep back straight and chest up as you lift
  • Drive hips forward to stand up with the weight
  • Lower with control, maintaining proper form

UPPER BODY DEVELOPMENT

Weighted Pull-ups

4 sets of 6-10 reps

Build a powerful, V-tapered back with this fundamental upper body exercise. Essential for complete back development.

  • Grip bar slightly wider than shoulder-width
  • Pull yourself up until chin clears the bar
  • Squeeze shoulder blades together at the top
  • Lower yourself with control to full extension
  • Add weight with dip belt for progressive overload
Lats Biceps Rear Delts Forearms

Standing Overhead Press

4 sets of 6-8 reps

Develop powerful shoulders and core stability with this compound movement. Builds impressive shoulder mass and strength.

  • Stand with feet shoulder-width apart
  • Grip barbell at shoulder height with palms forward
  • Press bar overhead until arms are fully extended
  • Keep core tight and avoid excessive back arch
  • Lower with control to starting position

Bent-Over Barbell Rows

4 sets of 8-10 reps

Build thickness and density in your back muscles. Essential for developing a powerful, complete back.

  • Stand with feet shoulder-width apart
  • Hinge at hips until torso is near parallel to floor
  • Pull barbell to lower chest/upper abdomen
  • Squeeze shoulder blades together at peak contraction
  • Lower with control and repeat

ARMS & SHOULDERS

Barbell Curls

3 sets of 8-12 reps

The classic bicep builder for adding mass and peak to your arms. Essential for developing impressive bicep size.

  • Stand holding barbell with underhand grip
  • Keep elbows pinned to your sides
  • Curl weight up toward shoulders
  • Squeeze biceps hard at top of movement
  • Lower with control, don't use momentum
Biceps Forearms

Close-Grip Bench Press

3 sets of 8-10 reps

Build massive triceps while maintaining chest engagement. The most effective compound movement for tricep development.

  • Lie on bench with hands shoulder-width apart
  • Lower bar to lower chest/mid-sternum
  • Keep elbows tucked close to body
  • Press bar back to starting position
  • Focus on tricep contraction throughout movement
Triceps Chest Shoulders

Dumbbell Lateral Raises

3 sets of 12-15 reps

Develop broad, impressive shoulders with this isolation exercise. Essential for creating that coveted V-taper.

  • Stand holding dumbbells at your sides
  • Raise arms out to sides until parallel to floor
  • Keep slight bend in elbows throughout movement
  • Lower with control, don't let weights drop
  • Focus on shoulder contraction, not weight
