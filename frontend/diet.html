Nutrition Basics

Before you pick a plan, understand the science behind nutrition and how your body uses food for energy and recovery.

Calories (Energy)

To lose weight, you must eat fewer calories than you burn (Deficit). To gain muscle, you need slightly more (Surplus). Track your daily intake for optimal results.

Macros

Protein rebuilds muscle. Carbs provide energy for workouts. Fats regulate hormones. Balancing these is key to achieving your fitness goals efficiently.

Hydration

Water is crucial for metabolism and nutrient transport. Aim for 3-4 liters a day to keep your body functioning at peak performance and aid recovery.

Choose Your Path

Select a nutrition strategy that fits your lifestyle, preferences, and goals. Each plan includes detailed guidance and meal suggestions.

Keto / Low Carb

Goal: Rapid Fat Loss

Drastically reduces carbs to force your body into "Ketosis," where it burns stored body fat for fuel instead of sugar. Effective for quick weight loss.

  • Fatty Fish & Salmon
  • Avocados & Olive Oil
  • Eggs & Cheese
  • Leafy Greens

High Protein

Goal: Muscle Gain

Focuses on high protein intake (1g per lb of bodyweight) to repair muscle tears after heavy lifting sessions and support muscle growth.

  • Chicken Breast
  • Lean Beef
  • Greek Yogurt
  • Whey Protein

Mediterranean

Goal: Heart Health

Voted the healthiest diet in the world. Focuses on whole foods, healthy fats, and unprocessed ingredients for long-term health benefits.

  • Whole Grains
  • Olive Oil
  • Tomatoes & Veggies
  • Fish & Poultry

Plant Based

Goal: Ethics & Energy

A diet free from all animal products. Rich in fiber, vitamins, and antioxidants. Requires careful protein tracking to ensure nutritional adequacy.

  • Lentils & Beans
  • Tofu & Tempeh
  • Quinoa
  • Nuts & Seeds

Paleo

Goal: Natural Eating

The "Caveman" diet. If you can't hunt it or gather it, don't eat it. Avoids processed grains, dairy, and sugar for a back-to-basics approach.

  • Grass-Fed Meat
  • Fish & Seafood
  • Fruits & Nuts
  • No Grains or Dairy

Intermittent Fasting

Goal: Discipline

Not about *what* you eat, but *when*. Usually involves eating all meals within an 8-hour window (e.g., 12pm - 8pm) to improve metabolic health.

  • 16 Hours Fasting
  • 8 Hours Eating
  • Black Coffee (During Fast)
  • Water (During Fast)
